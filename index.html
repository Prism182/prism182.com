What do I do?

My creations are often random and spontaneously made, dependent on what currently interests me; hence I have little real structure to my work.
I often work in C#, Java (which I am self-taught in), python, and really just any other programming language I find that I have an idea to make something in; typically, I do not work constantly on the same project; my main method of choosing what to work is to wait until I get the spontaneous motivation to create something new!

EMS

EMS is a minecraft mod which aims to provide minecraft with truly realistic lighting.
Standing for "Electromagnetic Spectrum", It uses the veil rendering engine, and a LOT of maths to simulate all wavelengths of light.
This spans from Radio waves all the way to Gamma rays!
